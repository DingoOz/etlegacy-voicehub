⚙ settingsconnecting

Hear your team's bots talk and talk back to them. Pick your in-game name, then hold the big button, press your in-game key, or leave the mic open.

First visit: your browser will warn about the self-signed certificate. Choose Advanced → proceed once; it is the LAN server, not the internet.

Space bar works too. Say a bot's name to talk to that bot.
In-game keys

Open the game console (~) once and paste:

bind v "cmd vh_talk"
bind b "cmd vh_mic"

V starts listening on this page and sends when you stop talking (press again to send at once). B toggles always-listening. Keep this page open in the background; it must have picked your name.
